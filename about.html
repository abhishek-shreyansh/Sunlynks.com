About SunLynks

SunLynks is a PAN-India solar consulting and solutions company committed to simplifying the way individuals and businesses adopt solar energy. We help customers make confident decisions through transparent consulting, accurate system sizing, brand comparisons, technical guidance, and end-to-end project coordination.

Our approach is simple—clarity, trust, and performance. We work closely with top solar brands, EPC companies, and equipment manufacturers to provide reliable, cost-effective, and high-quality solutions tailored to each client's requirements. Whether it's a small residential setup or a full-scale industrial project, SunLynks ensures that every step—from feasibility to installation—is handled with expertise and precision.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower India to move towards a cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable energy future by making solar energy easy, efficient, and accessible for all.
